Important Fee Information
  • All professional fees are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and exclude applicable taxes (GST/QST/HST where applicable).
  • Government fees are paid directly to IRCC or provincial authorities and are subject to change without notice.
  • Disbursements (courier, translations, certified copies) are billed separately.
  • Complex cases, rush filings, or additional dependants may incur additional fees — discussed during consultation.
  • A written Retainer Agreement outlining scope and fees is required before services commence.
  • Consultation fees may be credited toward your file if you retain us for the same matter.
