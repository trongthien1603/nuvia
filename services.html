Our Immigration Services

Expert guidance across all major Canadian immigration pathways.

🍁

Express Entry

Canada's flagship economic immigration system for skilled workers. We help you:

  • Create and optimize your Express Entry profile
  • Improve your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score
  • Prepare for Invitation to Apply (ITA)
  • Submit complete permanent residence applications
🏛️

Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP)

Provincial streams offer additional pathways to permanent residence:

  • Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP)
  • BC Provincial Nominee Program (BC PNP)
  • Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP)
  • Other provincial and territorial programs
🎓

Study & Work Permits

Support for temporary residents pursuing education and employment in Canada:

  • Study permit applications and extensions
  • Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)
  • LMIA and LMIA-exempt work permits
  • Intra-company transfers and IEC
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Sponsorship

Reunite with loved ones through family class immigration:

  • Spousal and common-law partner sponsorship
  • Dependent children sponsorship
  • Parent and grandparent sponsorship (PGP)
  • Super visa applications
💼

Business Immigration

Pathways for entrepreneurs and investors:

  • Start-Up Visa Program
  • Self-Employed Persons Program
  • Provincial entrepreneur streams
  • Owner-operator LMIA pathways
⚖️

Refusals, Appeals & Complex Cases

Strategic support when applications don't go as planned:

  • Application refusal analysis
  • Reconsideration and resubmission strategies
  • Judicial review referrals
  • Restoration of status applications
