Thien Pham

Owner & Founder | RCIC-IRB | Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant

Thien Pham is the Owner and Founder of Nuvia Immigration Consulting and a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) recognized by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC). He helps individuals and families navigate Canada's immigration system with clarity, integrity, and care.

Mr. Pham holds a Class L3 RCIC-IRB — Unrestricted Practice licence (CICC #R1040752), authorizing representation before Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada (IRB).

He specializes in economic immigration, provincial nominee programs, study and work permits, family sponsorship, and refusal or restoration cases — providing strategic guidance tailored to each client's background and goals.

Fluent in English, French, and Vietnamese, Mr. Pham is committed to helping clients from diverse backgrounds understand complex immigration processes with confidence. You are welcome to verify his credentials at any time on the CICC public register.
